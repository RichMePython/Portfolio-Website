About Julius

Computer science applied to real financial decisions.

Julius Marandure is a Part 2 Computer Science student at the University of Zimbabwe with a strong focus on AI, fintech, automation, simulation, and financial decision systems.

A finance-focused technology builder.

Julius builds systems that connect computer science with financial problems where better data, smarter models, and clearer decision support can make a practical difference. His work focuses on credit risk analysis, loan default prediction, financial market intelligence, data extraction from complex online platforms, automated opportunity detection, and AI-powered financial modelling.

He is especially interested in agentic AI systems, Monte Carlo simulation, borrower behaviour modelling, alternative market data, predictive analytics, and dashboards that make technical outputs understandable for business users.

This background is relevant to modern financial services because finance depends on trustworthy digital tools that can understand customers, model risk, automate analysis, and support smarter outcomes across banking, lending, insurance, savings, asset management, and customer experience.

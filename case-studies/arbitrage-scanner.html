AI-Powered Betting Market Arbitrage Scanner

An intelligent web-based system that reads betting websites, extracts odds from multiple sources, compares pricing, and identifies arbitrage opportunities through probability calculations.

Core idea

Read fragmented market prices, convert them into comparable probabilities, and identify gaps that suggest a risk-balanced opportunity.

Project Overview

The AI-Powered Betting Market Arbitrage Scanner is a web-based system that reads betting websites using web scrapers and AI reading techniques. It scans sports betting markets, extracts odds, compares prices across sources, calculates implied probabilities, and identifies arbitrage opportunities where market price differences can support risk-balanced outcomes.

Problem Statement

Betting and prediction markets often contain fragmented pricing data across platforms. Manually comparing odds is slow, repetitive, error-prone, and difficult to scale. The project solves this by automating extraction, comparison, and opportunity detection.

Why the Project Matters

Although the project uses betting market data, the underlying concept is highly relevant to financial markets: gather prices, normalize them, detect inefficiencies, calculate risk, and present decision-ready opportunities. These are the same analytical muscles used in market intelligence and pricing analysis.

System Architecture

The system architecture reads online market data, structures it, converts odds into probabilities, and surfaces opportunities through a dashboard.

Betting Websites-> Web Scraper / AI Reader-> Odds Extraction-> Probability Conversion-> Arbitrage Calculator-> Opportunity Dashboard

Data Flow

1. Source readingBetting website content is read through scrapers and AI-assisted readers.
2. Event extractionSports events, teams, markets, and odds are converted into structured records.
3. Market comparisonEquivalent markets are matched across sources for comparison.
4. Probability logicOdds are converted into implied probabilities and tested for arbitrage gaps.
5. Opportunity scoringArbitrage percentages and risk-balanced outputs are calculated.
6. Dashboard outputOpportunities are displayed with odds, percentages, and supporting results.

AI / Simulation Approach

The project uses AI reading techniques to interpret online content that may be difficult to parse with rigid selectors alone. Probability calculations then convert extracted odds into comparable numbers. The arbitrage logic evaluates whether combined probabilities create a favourable gap.

Key Features

  • Reads betting website content using web scrapers and AI readers.
  • Extracts available sports events and odds.
  • Compares odds across markets and sources.
  • Calculates arbitrage opportunities and opportunity percentages.
  • Displays odds, arbitrage percentages, and opportunity results in a dashboard UI.

Results or Expected Impact

The project demonstrates how automated systems can scan fragmented markets faster than manual review, identify pricing inefficiencies, and support structured financial-style analysis. Its broader value is showing Julius's ability to build market monitoring systems that combine data extraction, logic, and dashboard presentation.

Lessons Learned

Julius learned that market data is often messy, inconsistent, and difficult to compare without careful extraction and normalization. The project strengthened his understanding of probability, data quality, pricing gaps, and the need for clear opportunity presentation.

Future Improvements

  • Add notification workflows for high-confidence opportunities.
  • Extend the dashboard with historical opportunity tracking and risk notes.

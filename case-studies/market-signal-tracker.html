A public-market intelligence tool that monitors public activity patterns on prediction market platforms, studies selected high-performing participants, and turns behaviour signals into decision-support insight.

Core idea

Use public activity data to identify behavioural patterns from experienced participants and convert those signals into market intelligence.

Project Overview

The Prediction Market Insider Signal Tracker is a market intelligence application that monitors public activity patterns on prediction market platforms such as Polymarket. The system tracks selected high-performing traders, studies public decision patterns, and uses those signals to support market insight and automated decision-making.

Problem Statement

Prediction markets generate valuable behavioural and sentiment signals, but identifying useful patterns from experienced participants is difficult manually. Public market actions can be noisy, fragmented, and hard to interpret without structured tracking.

Why the Project Matters

Financial decision-making increasingly uses alternative data and behavioural signals. This project explores how public actions in prediction markets can be monitored, structured, and interpreted as market intelligence. The focus is ethical public data analysis, not private or unlawful information.

System Architecture

The system architecture turns public prediction market activity into pattern analysis and decision-support outputs.

Public Prediction Market Data-> Trader Activity Tracking-> Behaviour Pattern Analysis-> Signal Detection-> Decision-Support Output

Data Flow

1. Public data intakePrediction market activity and public position changes are monitored.
2. Participant selectionHigh-performing or strategically interesting public participants are selected for tracking.
3. Behaviour mappingThe system studies timing, market choice, position changes, and confidence patterns.
4. Signal extractionRepeated patterns are converted into market intelligence signals.
5. Insight scoringSignals are ranked by relevance, consistency, and possible decision value.
6. Decision outputThe final dashboard supports market research and decision-making.

AI / Simulation Approach

The application uses automated readers and tracking logic to monitor public market activity. Behaviour pattern analysis identifies recurring decisions and possible signals from selected participants. The system can then support decision-making by highlighting patterns that would be difficult to identify manually.

Key Features

  • Tracks activity from selected high-performing public market participants.
  • Reads public market actions and position changes.
  • Identifies patterns in trading behaviour and decision timing.
  • Supports decision-making based on market intelligence signals.
  • Demonstrates alternative data analysis and behavioural finance insight.

Results or Expected Impact

The expected impact is more systematic interpretation of public market behaviour. The system can help users understand sentiment, participant confidence, and decision patterns in prediction markets, turning scattered public activity into clearer research signals.

Lessons Learned

Julius learned that alternative data needs careful framing, ethical boundaries, and strong interpretation. Public data can still be noisy, so decision-support tools must separate useful signals from coincidence and avoid overstating certainty.

Future Improvements

  • Add stronger signal confidence scoring and false-positive filtering.
  • Track signal performance over time to validate usefulness.
  • Improve dashboards with market sentiment summaries and alerting.
  • Add explainability notes for why a public signal is highlighted.

Relevance to Financial Services

This project demonstrates knowledge of alternative financial data, behavioural finance, market sentiment, trading signals, and automated market intelligence. It shows Julius's ability to build tools that monitor public data, detect patterns, and translate market behaviour into decision-support insight.

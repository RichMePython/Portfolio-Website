Project 1

AI-Powered Betting Market Arbitrage Scanner

An intelligent web-based system that reads betting websites using a combination of web scraping and AI reading techniques. The app scans sports betting markets, extracts odds from multiple sources, and identifies arbitrage opportunities where price differences can be used to create risk-balanced outcomes.

Problem solvedBetting and prediction markets contain fragmented pricing data across platforms. Manually comparing odds is slow, error-prone, and inefficient.
Technologies usedWeb scraping, AI readers, data extraction, probability calculations, arbitrage logic, dashboard UI, and automation.
Key featuresReads website content, extracts events and odds, compares markets, calculates arbitrage opportunities, and displays opportunity results.
Financial relevanceDemonstrates financial market analysis, pricing inefficiency detection, arbitrage awareness, and automated opportunity discovery.
ImpactHelps demonstrate real-time financial scanning and market inefficiency detection.

Agentic AI Loan Default Prediction System

An advanced AI-driven credit risk prediction system that uses AI agentic swarm behaviour, Monte Carlo simulations, and traditional AI models to predict loan defaulters with high success. The system simulates borrower behaviour under different financial conditions and produces risk insights for lending decisions.

Problem solvedTraditional loan default prediction often relies only on historical data and static models. This system combines predictive AI with simulation-based behavioural modelling.
Technologies usedAgentic AI, AI swarm systems, Monte Carlo simulation, machine learning, credit risk modelling, behavioural simulation, and predictive analytics.
Key featuresSimulates borrower behaviour, tests repayment scenarios, predicts probability of default, and supports lending risk assessment.
Financial relevanceThe strongest finance fit: banking, lending, credit risk, customer analytics, financial inclusion, and responsible lending.
